# Changelog: fix/update-design-repweights-count

**Branch:** `fix/update-design-repweights-count`
**Status:** Complete
**Date:** 2026-10-21
**Issue:** #300

## Summary

Fixes #300. `update_design(x, repweights = )` on a `survey_replicate` design
could change the number of replicate columns. It replaced
`@variables$repweights` and nothing else, so `scale`, `rscales` and
`fpc_fraction` stayed as they were set for the old replicate count. For JK1,
BRR, Fay, bootstrap, ACS and successive-difference the default scale is a
function of `R`, so every later standard error used the wrong factor, and
nothing signalled it. `rscales` and `fpc_fraction` have one entry per
replicate, so a changed `R` could also leave them the wrong length.

`update_design()` now refuses a `repweights` update that changes the
replicate count, with `surveycore_error_repweights_count_changed`, and points
the caller to `as_survey_replicate()`. An update that keeps the count still
works and keeps all three keys as they are.

### Why refuse rather than recompute

The design does not record whether its `scale` was the type's default or a
value the caller supplied, so recomputing it would mean guessing. The refusal
applies to every replicate type, including JK2, JKn and `other`, whose scale
does not depend on `R`: `rscales` still has one entry per replicate for those
types, and a single rule is easier to document than a per-type one.

## Verification

Measured before the fix, JK1, `n = 60`, 10 replicates, seed 11, reduced to 4
replicate columns:

| Design | stored `scale` | SE of `y1` |
|---|--:|--:|
| `as_survey_replicate()` on the 4 columns | 0.75 | 0.1709033 |
| `update_design()` from 10 to 4 columns | 0.9 | 0.1872152 |

The ratio is `sqrt(0.9 / 0.75)`, a 9.5% overstatement. After the fix the
second call raises UD-1.

New test blocks:

- Fewer columns is refused: dual pattern, `class =` and snapshot
- More columns is refused: `class =`
- A refused update leaves the design's `@variables` unchanged
- A same-count JK1 update matches a fresh `as_survey_replicate()` build on
  the new columns: `scale` and the `get_means()` standard error at 1e-8
